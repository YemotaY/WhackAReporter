# 🔨 WHACK-A-REPORTER

> **Bonk the press. Dodge the questions. Get re-elected.**

## > THE BRIEFING

You are da Präsident — the greatest president, possibly of all time.
Reporters keep popping up behind podiums with *serious questions*.
Bonk them with your presidential gavel before they finish asking, or your
approval rating tanks. Survive the full 20-minute term to get **RE-ELECTED**.

## > FEATURES

- 🔨 Fully voiced satirical Q&A — hear every question once, then it's pure whacking chaos
- 🔨 10 levels of escalating madness: moving podiums, bobbing holes, midterm panic
- 🔨 Neural-TTS presidential one-liners: "Fake news!" "Next question!" "Wrong!"
- 🔨 Arcade high-score leaderboard with 3-letter initials, like the good old days
- 🔨 CRT scanline overlay for maximum 198X energy
- 🔨 Made with Godot — free & open source
